Common questions

Before you ask.

How do I get to Skardu?

By air from Islamabad, weather permitting, or by road through the mountains. We can arrange pick-up from the airport or plan the road journey with you.

When is the best time to visit?

It depends on what you want to see. Summer is best for Deosai and treks, spring for blossoms, autumn for colour and winter for snow. Our seasons page has the details.

Can you plan trips for families?

Yes. We keep drives short, choose child-friendly stays and leave plenty of free time.

Do some trips need permits?

Some treks and areas do. Tell us where you want to go and we'll let you know what's needed and help you arrange it.

How do I book and pay?

Call or WhatsApp us on , or send a request from the trip planner. Tell us your dates and group size and pick a Standard, Deluxe or Executive package. We confirm the plan and how to pay with you directly.
